Seven control questions

Score the evidence chain.

Choose the answer that most closely describes current operating reality—not the target state.

1. Can you identify the exact fee authority and rule version that governed a historical permit?

2. Are the permit/project facts used to calculate fees available in a structured export?

3. Can permits, assessed fees, payments, and ERP postings be reliably joined across systems?

4. Do credits, exemptions, waivers, refunds, and reimbursements retain supporting evidence?

5. Can a receipt be traced to its accounting posting and expected restricted-fund destination?

6. Are prior discrepancies and corrections retained in a way that supports pattern analysis?

7. Is ownership explicit for reviewing financial, legal, policy, and accounting exceptions?
