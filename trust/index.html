TRUST & PROCUREMENT

Separate what is live, what is demonstrable, and what requires commissioning.

Tallyridge’s public trust posture is intentionally evidence-bound. This page distinguishes the hardened GTM environment from controls that belong to a future agency-specific production deployment.

Current public deployment
LIVE

Public GTM surface

Marketing, baseline scope, readiness scan, synthetic demo, trust documentation, security headers, health check, sitemap, and security.txt are deployed on Vercel.

LIVE

Synthetic assurance demo

Finding classifications, evidence labels, filters, and downloadable synthetic CSV can be inspected without exposing agency data.

LIVE

Local request builder

Agencies can generate a procurement-ready baseline request brief locally without transmitting municipal or contact information.

NOT COMMISSIONED

Municipal production data plane

No production database, agency tenant, identity provider, source-system connector, or municipal record ingestion pipeline is represented as live.

NOT COMMISSIONED

Enterprise identity & retention

SSO, MFA, RBAC, tenant isolation, retention schedules, customer-managed keys, backup, DR, and incident response require deployment-specific commissioning.

NOT COMMISSIONED

Lead-capture backend

No CRM, intake mailbox, or secure submission destination is fabricated on this public deployment. The request builder is local-only until a verified destination exists.

Procurement boundary

Agency-specific controls must be proven in the commissioned environment.

Claims about production identity, encryption architecture, retention, connector security, backup, incident response, logging, tenant isolation, or data residency should be supported by deployment-specific evidence—not copied from the public GTM site.

05
