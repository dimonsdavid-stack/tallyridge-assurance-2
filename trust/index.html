PROCUREMENT INFORMATION

Information for agency review and purchasing.

A development fee review can begin with a defined scope, available agency records, responsible departments, and the agency’s applicable purchasing and information-security requirements.

Before engagement

Scope

Identify the fee programs, departments, fiscal years, and questions to be reviewed.

Records

Confirm which adopted fee documents, permit records, payment records, and accounting records are available.

Department contacts

Identify Finance, Community Development, Public Works, Legal, Internal Audit, and information technology contacts as applicable.

Agency requirements

Confirm purchasing, contracting, insurance, records handling, information security, and retention requirements.
