CIVICRECONCILE · LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Control what you share.

Public tools process files locally. Review requests send only the contact and scope fields you choose to submit.

Document and CSV tools

Files are read in browser memory and are not uploaded to our servers. Clear the form or close the tab to discard the local results. Downloaded outputs remain on your device. Do not use a shared device for agency-approved confidential records.

Review requests

Submitting sends agency, office, work email, jurisdiction, available-record descriptions and requested scope to private request storage for evaluation and response. Do not include permits, personal records, payment details or credentials. The request is not a purchase or consent to marketing.

Website hosting may process ordinary request metadata. No third-party advertising or tracking scripts are installed. Agency production data transfer, retention, access, incident handling and disposition are established in a written agreement before collection. Contact and scope requests expire after 90 days under the daily retention job. Rate-limit markers expire after 48 hours. Failed cleanup is an operational issue to resolve; the account operator can delete records through private storage administration.
