CIVICRECONCILE · LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Make the purchasing decision reviewable.

A clear scope, price basis and evidence package help an agency select its lawful procurement route.

01

Agency-approved purchase

Purchasing confirms the applicable threshold, quotations, professional-services rules, budget authority and contract approver. A small pilot is not a reason to split a larger purchase to evade competition.

02

Competitive or cooperative route

For cooperative purchasing, verify an actual competed award, eligible scope, participation terms, current term, prices and agency authority. No CivicReconcile cooperative contract is currently claimed.

03

Document the decision

Attach the statement of work, commercial terms, reviewer ownership, data plan, insurance requirements and acceptance criteria. Deployment timing follows authorization and records readiness.

Cooperative scale pathway

Secure a genuine competitive anchor award with suitable participation terms. Publish the solicitation, award, scope, pricing, eligibility and expiration only after an executed award. Each joining agency checks its own authority; a piggyback clause alone does not confer it.

Recovery agreement controls

Agree baseline receivables, exclusions, cash receipts, refund/appeal adjustments, fee cap and attribution before work. Compensation cannot turn accounting corrections into recovery or override restricted-fund uses. Independently review disputed and adverse findings.
