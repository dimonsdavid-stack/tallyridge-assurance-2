Scope a Review
CIVICRECONCILE · LOCAL GOVERNMENT

A difference is a question to resolve.

Reproduce the calculation, locate the supporting record and assign the determination to an authorized reviewer.

1. Preserve the basis

Record source files, hashes, review period, applicable dates and supplied authority version. Capture known omissions before comparison.

2. Compare deterministically

Apply the supplied schedule and credits, compare assessment and collection amounts, and test the supplied fund crosswalk. Overlapping authority periods and duplicates are not evaluated.

3. Review with named staff

Finance and program staff confirm mappings, adjustments and payment timing. Agency counsel resolves vesting, agreements, exemptions, collectibility and disputed authority.

4. Preserve the conclusion

Retain the evidence, calculation, reviewer, resolution and realized outcome. No chargeback, refund or collection notice is issued by a public tool.

What the software does

The available local tools extract searchable PDF context and compare supplied CSV records using explicit rules. They produce evidence references and downloadable registers. They do not autonomously determine legal liability.

Who performs professional review

Agency-designated Finance, Internal Audit and Legal staff review conclusions. Any external accountant or attorney, credentials, scope, insurance and deliverable are named in a separate engagement. No forensic-accounting staff or licensed assurance engagement is represented as already retained.

This is a reconciliation and decision-support service. A licensed audit opinion, nexus study or legal opinion requires a separately qualified professional engagement.
