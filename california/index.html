CIVICRECONCILE · LOCAL GOVERNMENT

Start with California development fees.

A focused pathway for Finance, Internal Audit and City or County administration, using the agency’s adopted authority and records.

Authority and program boundaries

Government Code §66001 addresses fee purpose, use and reasonable relationships. Applicability, vesting, agreements and credits require agency review. Utility capacity charges, school fees and other programs may follow different rules; they are scoped separately.

Read §66001

Fund and reporting evidence

Review separate fee accounts, the annual fee report, receipts, expenditures, interest, transfers, refunds and applicable five-year findings. The ACFR is an entry point, not a substitute for the fee-program ledger and reporting record.

Read §66006

First engagement

Choose one transportation or parks impact-fee program, the last three completed fiscal years, and a Finance sponsor. Expand only after the source mapping and exception workflow are reproducible. Internal Audit and the Assistant City Manager for Finance are appropriate sponsors; permit staff remain essential implementation partners.

Review both under-assessments and overcharges. A fund correction is not new revenue. A reported balance is not automatically collectible.

Build the agency packet

  • Adopted resolutions, schedules and effective-date amendments
  • Nexus study, agreements, credit and exemption authority
  • Permit/assessment extract and collection/adjustment history
  • Fee-program-to-fund crosswalk and general ledger
  • Annual fee reports, five-year findings and improvement schedules
  • Agency staff, counsel and procurement sign-off
