One assurance layer · five explicit control surfaces

The development-revenue assurance stack.

Do not replace permitting, cashiering, ERP, or document systems. Reconcile the records they already produce and preserve a defensible evidence path across them.

01 · AUTHORITY

Rule Library

Version resolutions, schedules, nexus materials, agreements, effective dates, credits, exemptions, and waivers.

02 · ASSESSMENT

Expected Fee Engine

Reconstruct the fee that should have been assessed from authoritative facts and historical rule versions.

03 · COLLECTION

Cash Reconciliation

Match assessed obligations to receipts, reversals, refunds, write-offs, and unresolved balances.

04 · ACCOUNTING

Ledger Assurance

Trace cash into the expected funds and identify unmatched, misclassified, or incomplete postings.

05 · PROOF

Evidence Pack

Preserve sources, rule versions, calculations, reviewer disposition, and exportable finding evidence.

The operating layer your source systems do not provide

Reconcile the transaction chain instead of auditing each system in isolation.

Permit systems show permits. Cashiering systems show receipts. ERPs show postings. Policy files show authority. Tallyridge joins the operating truth without pretending any one source is complete.

RV

Historical rule versioning

Bind every calculation to the rule version, date, classification, project facts, and exception authority that applied at the time.

EA

Expected-vs-actual assessment

Recalculate expected obligations and classify differences before anyone labels them collectible, refundable, or immaterial.

CL

Collection lineage

Trace assessed amounts into payment records, reversals, refunds, credits, and open receivables.

GL

General-ledger matching

Connect receipts to accounting entries and expected restricted funds without confusing presence of cash with correct posting.

DG

Disposition governance

Separate confirmed discrepancies, potential findings, overcollections, policy opportunities, and no-exception records.

EP

Evidence-grade exports

Make the basis for each conclusion reproducible by finance, legal, planning, public works, audit, and external reviewers.

The five-pillar municipal assurance model

Authority. Assessment. Collection. Ledger. Proof.

01

Authority

Resolve which ordinance, resolution, study, agreement, schedule, exemption, credit, and effective date governs the transaction.

02

Assessment

Calculate the expected fee and compare it to the amount actually assessed on the project or permit record.

03

Collection

Verify what was paid, refunded, reversed, credited, waived, written off, or left outstanding.

04

Ledger

Verify the accounting posting and expected restricted-fund destination using the available evidence population.

05

Proof

Package source lineage, calculations, evidence gaps, assumptions, and human disposition so the conclusion can be reproduced.
