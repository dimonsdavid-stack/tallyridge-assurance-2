WHAT TALLYRIDGE CHECKS

Assurance, not another system of record.

Permitting platforms calculate. ERP platforms account. Fee studies establish policy. Tallyridge is designed to independently reconcile whether those records agree end-to-end.

Historical rule reconstruction

Version fee schedules, resolutions, effective dates, credits, exemptions, waivers, and developer agreements.

Expected-vs-actual assessment

Recalculate expected charges from authoritative inputs and compare them with assessed transactions.

Cash & ledger matching

Trace payments into accounting entries and restricted funds to detect unmatched or misclassified activity.

Evidence-grade disposition

Separate confirmed discrepancies from potential findings, overpayments, policy opportunities, and no-exception records.
