What we review

One review across the records that determine whether development revenue was handled correctly.

01 · FEE AUTHORITY

Adopted fees and effective dates

Ordinances, resolutions, fee schedules, nexus studies, development agreements, credits, exemptions, and waivers.

02 · ASSESSMENT & COLLECTION

Amounts charged and paid

Compare permit and project records with assessed fees, payments, refunds, reversals, credits, and outstanding amounts.

03 · ACCOUNTING

General ledger and fund posting

Trace collections to accounting entries and the applicable restricted or designated fund.

04 · REVIEW RECORD

Findings for staff review

Document the record, applicable fee schedule, amount reviewed, supporting material, and department responsible for follow-up.

For department leadership

Answer the questions that matter before the next audit, budget cycle, or fee update.

  • Were adopted fees applied using the correct schedule and effective date?
  • Do assessed amounts agree with permit and project records?
  • Do collections agree with receipts and accounting entries?
  • Were credits, exemptions, waivers, and refunds supported?
  • Were revenues posted to the appropriate fund?
