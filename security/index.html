INFORMATION SECURITY

Agency records should be handled according to the scope and sensitivity of the review.

The public Tallyridge website contains illustrative information only. Agency records are not submitted through the public website.

Public website

No production agency records, passwords, or system credentials are stored in this public website.

Minimum necessary records

A review should request only the records needed for the approved scope and period.

Access by responsibility

Access to agency records should be limited to personnel whose duties require the information.

Agency determinations

Agency staff retain responsibility for legal, accounting, refund, exemption, and policy determinations.

Before records are provided
  • Confirm the approved scope and review period
  • Identify the records and fields required
  • Identify authorized agency contacts
  • Establish the approved method for record transfer
  • Confirm retention and disposition requirements
  • Document any confidentiality or access restrictions
For agency information technology review
  • Access controls and user responsibilities
  • Encryption requirements
  • Record retention and deletion
  • Backup and recovery requirements
  • Security incident procedures
  • Contract and procurement requirements
Procurement and records handling

Review the agency requirements before transferring records.
