30–45 DAY ENGAGEMENT

Impact Fee Assurance Baseline

Establish whether cross-system discrepancies are material, actionable, and recurring before committing to continuous assurance.

01

Scope & authority map

Define fee programs, historical periods, source systems, authoritative resolutions, nexus materials, and review owners.

02

Data normalization

Load permit, assessment, payment, ERP, credit, waiver, reimbursement, and selected fund data into a reconciled model.

03

Rule reconstruction

Version fee rules by effective date and model the specific facts required to calculate an expected assessment.

04

Reconciliation

Compare expected assessment → actual assessment → cash → accounting posting and classify exceptions.

05

Disposition workshop

Agency finance, legal, planning, public works, and audit stakeholders review evidence before findings are finalized.

06

Executive evidence pack

Deliver a reproducible finding register, control gaps, data-quality findings, and a recommendation on continuous assurance.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Start with exports, not a rip-and-replace integration.

  • Permit population and project attributes
  • Assessed fee transactions
  • Historical fee schedules and resolutions
  • Payment records
  • ERP / general-ledger transactions
  • Credits, waivers, reimbursements
  • Fund balance and selected CIP data where relevant
FINDING TAXONOMY

Every exception gets a defensible classification.

  • Confirmed discrepancy
  • Potential discrepancy
  • Overcollection / refund candidate
  • Accounting classification exception
  • Policy / update opportunity
  • Restricted-fund reporting risk
  • CIP eligibility candidate
  • No exception
