Review steps
01

Confirm scope

Identify fee programs, departments, fiscal years, governing documents, and agency staff responsible for review.

02

Review records

Compare adopted fees with permit assessments, payments, adjustments, refunds, credits, and accounting entries.

03

Report findings

Provide confirmed differences, items requiring staff determination, records with no exception, and recommended follow-up.

Records typically requested
  • Adopted fee schedules, ordinances, and resolutions
  • Nexus studies and development agreements
  • Permit and project records
  • Fee assessments and adjustments
  • Payment, refund, and reversal records
  • Credits, waivers, exemptions, and reimbursements
  • General ledger transaction detail
  • Restricted-fund records
Findings provided
  • Confirmed assessment difference
  • Item requiring department review
  • Possible overpayment or refund review
  • Unmatched receipt or accounting entry
  • Unsupported credit, waiver, or exemption
  • Fund posting requiring review
  • Missing supporting record
  • No exception
Deliverables

Executive summary

Scope, records reviewed, material findings, unresolved items, and recommended next steps.

Detailed findings

Record-level findings with the applicable fee authority, amounts reviewed, and supporting documentation.

Records requiring follow-up

Items requiring Finance, Community Development, Public Works, Legal, or other department determination.

Control recommendations

Practical changes to procedures, documentation, review responsibilities, or recurring reconciliation.

For cities, counties, districts, and public agencies.
