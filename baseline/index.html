Six-stage baseline

Start with exports. End with a reproducible evidence pack.

The baseline is intentionally bounded so municipal teams can validate source quality, rule chronology, exception economics, and review ownership before committing to a continuous program.

01 · SCOPE

Authority & population map

Define fee programs, historical periods, authoritative resolutions, nexus studies, agreements, source systems, and accountable reviewers.

02 · NORMALIZE

Cross-system record model

Align permit, assessment, payment, ERP, credit, waiver, reimbursement, fund, and selected capital-program records using stable identifiers.

03 · RECONSTRUCT

Historical rule chronology

Version rates, effective dates, classifications, credits, exemptions, vesting logic, waivers, and agreement-specific exceptions.

04 · RECONCILE

Expected vs. actual

Compare expected assessment → actual assessment → receipt → accounting posting while preserving evidence gaps and assumptions.

05 · DISPOSITION

Human review workshop

Finance, legal, planning, public works, audit, and other authorized owners classify findings before any financial conclusion is finalized.

06 · PROVE

Executive evidence pack

Deliver a reproducible finding register, control gaps, data-quality findings, economic evidence, and a go/no-go recommendation for continuous assurance.

Typical inputs

Begin with the records your agency already produces.

  • Permit population and project attributes
  • Assessed fee transactions and adjustments
  • Historical fee schedules and resolutions
  • Nexus studies and developer agreements
  • Payment, refund, reversal, and write-off records
  • ERP / general-ledger transaction detail
  • Credits, waivers, exemptions, reimbursements
  • Restricted-fund balances and selected CIP records
Finding taxonomy

Every exception gets a defensible disposition state.

  • Confirmed discrepancy
  • Potential discrepancy
  • Overcollection / refund candidate
  • Unmatched receipt or posting
  • Accounting classification exception
  • Policy / schedule update opportunity
  • Restricted-fund reporting risk
  • Evidence gap / insufficient support
  • No exception
Baseline outputs

What a decision-maker should receive at the end.

Not a dashboard full of unexplained anomalies. A bounded evidence package that says what was tested, what was found, what remains uncertain, and who must decide.

FR

Finding register

Record-level classifications with expected values, actual values, materiality, evidence status, and required reviewer.

RC

Rule chronology

Versioned schedule and authority map that makes calculation basis and effective-date logic inspectable.

DQ

Data-quality report

Missing identifiers, incomplete joins, inconsistent classifications, and populations that cannot yet support a defensible conclusion.

CG

Control-gap map

Where ownership, review, evidence retention, or source-system handoffs create recurring operational risk.

EV

Economic evidence

Only agency-reviewed collectible amounts, refund exposure, recurring leakage, and measurable labor effects are counted.

NX

Next-stage recommendation

Whether to stop, remediate source quality, rerun a focused test, or commission recurring assurance.
