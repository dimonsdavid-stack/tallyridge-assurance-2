Evidence standard

Every finding must be reproducible.

  • Source records and provenance
  • Applicable rule version and effective date
  • Formula and normalized inputs
  • Expected vs. actual values
  • Assumptions and unresolved evidence gaps
  • Required human disposition
Control principle

No autonomous financial disposition.

Final decisions involving collectibility, refunds, exemptions, legal authority, accounting corrections, or restricted use remain with authorized agency personnel and counsel.
