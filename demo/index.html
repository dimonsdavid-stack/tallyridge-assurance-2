Reproducible finding packet

  • Source records and provenance

  • Assumptions and unresolved evidence gaps

CONTROL PRINCIPLE

No autonomous financial disposition.

Tallyridge can surface and classify anomalies. Final decisions involving collectibility, refunds, exemptions, legal authority, accounting corrections, or restricted use remain with authorized agency personnel and counsel.
