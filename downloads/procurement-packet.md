# CivicReconcile — agency evaluation and purchasing package

## Purpose
Compare supplied development-fee authority, assessments, collections and fund records. Produce a traceable exception register for named staff review. No replacement citizen portal or ERP is required.

## Available public tools
Local searchable-PDF context check; local deterministic CSV reconciliation; source hashes and JSON/CSV exports; private contact/scope intake. Public tools do not receive uploaded documents or production records.

## Indicative commercial options
Public-document check $0. Focused baseline from $7,500: one program, one jurisdiction, up to three fiscal years and 2,500 supplied rows. Recurring monitoring from $18,000/year: up to three configured programs, 10,000 supplied rows/year and quarterly packages. Custom integrations, OCR and licensed professional work require separate scope. Final scope and price require an executed agreement.

## Statement-of-work fields
Agency and sponsor; program and fiscal years; authoritative documents; sources/mappings; row volume; known omissions; supplied-date basis; data classification; authorized transfer/access; named Finance/program/legal reviewers; acceptance tests; delivery timetable after authorization; retention/disposition; security/insurance requirements; payment basis and cap; incident/contact responsibilities.

## Acceptance tests
Reproduce sampled calculations from supplied source; record overlapping dates/duplicates as not evaluated; confirm fund crosswalk; preserve source hashes; count every supplied row; separate review candidates from agency-confirmed outcomes; maintain overcharge and missing-record findings; return export files that can be opened independently.

## Procurement route
Purchasing determines quotations, competition, professional-services selection or an eligible existing award. Cooperative use requires a real competed contract, eligible participation, matching scope/prices, valid term and agency approval. CivicReconcile does not claim an existing cooperative award, preferred vendor status or procurement exemption.

## Data and assurance boundary
The public website is not an authenticated municipal data system. Local tools are browser-based decision support. A commissioned agency workflow requires agreed identity, access, storage, security, retention, professional responsibilities and operating evidence before records are transferred. No CPA audit, nexus-study or legal opinion is bundled by implication.

## Commercial records to complete
Contracting entity and address; tax form; actual insurance certificates; named qualified personnel where needed; legal/signatory authority; final SLA; support channel; DPA/security exhibit; signed scope/order. These must be supplied by the operating business before accepting a municipal engagement.
