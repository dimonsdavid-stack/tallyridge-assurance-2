# CivicReconcile recovery-aligned engagement worksheet

Proposed terms for review, not an executed contract or legal opinion.

- Rate: 15% of agency-accepted eligible cash actually recovered; engagement cap $24,500.
- Eligibility: adopted, applicable authority; independently verified assessment; lawful collectibility; documented attribution; agency acceptance; approved procurement and payment source.
- Baseline: freeze existing receivables, scheduled payments and known collection activity before work.
- Exclusions: intra-agency fund transfers, classifications, ordinary expected payments, projections, refunds, avoided errors, amounts outside signed scope and duplicate findings.
- Adjustments: reduce the fee base for reversed receipts, successful appeals, refunds and agreed direct collection costs. Specify reconciliation and credit timing.
- No recovery fee if eligible recovery is zero. Identify any separate diagnostic fee or authorized expenses before work; do not describe paid work as free.
- No collection notices, refund decisions or developer communications without agency authority.
- Cap: cumulative billed success fees cannot exceed the signed engagement cap.
- Transition credit: define any annual-subscription credit and application date in the agreement; no automatic enrollment.
- Independence: disclose compensation to reviewers; keep adverse findings and overcharges in the register. Licensed assurance work must follow applicable professional independence rules.
- Agency counsel/purchasing review: confirm fee authority, restrictions, funding source, liability allocation, record retention, procurement route and required approvals.
- Finalization: both parties execute the scope and terms before record collection.
